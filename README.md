# Auto Chemical in Vietnam by Touchstone

**https://maxlu1980.github.io/Auto-Chemical-in-Vietnam-by-Touchstone/**

Interactive product guide for AUTO CHEMICAL INDUSTRY CO., LTD. (オート化学工業) waterproofing, sealant and adhesive products in Vietnam, published by **Touchstone Paint Group Ltd.**, authorized agent of AUTO CHEMICAL for Vietnam.

- Four product guides: UREAX series, PROTECT ONE, building sealants, building adhesives
- Technical data, application methods, coverage calculator and quotation in VND (VAT 10 %)
- Material submittal, method statement and quotation documents (Word / PDF)
- AUTO Việt Nam — a 3D walk from Tokyo to Saigon's Japan Town with training on each product
- 繁體中文 · English · 日本語 · Tiếng Việt
